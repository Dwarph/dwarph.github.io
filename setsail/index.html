Set Sail

A delightful screen recorder for macOS and Windows.

Select your platform and enter your email address

What it does

  • Records your whole screen, one window, or a bit you drag out.
  • Puts your webcam in a bubble on top, which you can move and resize mid-take.
  • Records your mic and your computer's own sound, each on or off whenever you like.
  • Makes transcripts on your own computer, and exports them as text.
  • Levels every take to a standard loudness, so nobody reaches for the volume.
  • Keeps its own windows out of the shot.

Questions

Is it free?

The beta is free. Leave your email and you'll get a link to install it, through TestFlight on a Mac or the Microsoft Store on Windows.

Do my recordings get uploaded anywhere?

No. Recordings save straight to a folder on your computer and stay there. There's no account to make. Crash reports and usage stats are off unless you choose to turn them on, and the privacy policy says exactly what they contain.

Can it record the sound my computer is playing?

Yes. System audio is built in on Windows and on macOS 13 or later, so there's no extra audio driver to install. You can switch it, and your mic, on or off in the middle of a take.

Are the transcripts private?

Yes. Transcripts are made on your own computer using Whisper, and nothing is sent anywhere.
